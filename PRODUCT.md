# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Composers, including the project's composer-researcher, working locally on a Mac alongside their DAW. The pilot targets Apple Silicon Macs with at least 16 GB RAM and uses Medium by default. Small remains an optional diagnostic.

## Product Purpose

Organize and describe recordings, verify machine suggestions and musical sections, fine-tune a personal model, listen to variations, and export audio or MIDI to a DAW. Audio and processing stay local after explicit model setup.

## Brand Personality

Calm, direct, composer-led. Use a restrained, light composition workspace: recordings, editable descriptions, and listening come first. Model controls use progressive disclosure.

## Anti-references

The earlier web interface was too confusing for this task. Avoid cloud administration controls and unexplained ML parameters in the main composition workflow.

## Design Principles

- Composer edits take priority over machine suggestions.
- Listen and verify before training; distinguish drafts from reviewed metadata.
- Describe the passage actually used for training.
- Preserve originals and make provenance visible.
- Run resource-intensive stages sequentially.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Continue labeled native controls, keyboard access, readable contrast, explicit text states, and reduced-motion support. Do not use color alone to communicate review status.

## Typography exclusions

User preference (3 November 2026): do not use Instrument Serif, Montserrat,
Playfair Display, DM Serif Display, Libre Baskerville, Cormorant Garamond,
Public Sans, Poppins (also written “poppin” in the reference), Lato,
Space Grotesk, or Schibsted Grotesk. Apply this to the app, web pages,
and generated review/listening pages. Keep typography local; current system-font
choices do not require a font download.

About this test

Each trial plays three short excerpts, A, B and C, cut from one piece of music. In each piece, one section was removed and regenerated by a model; the other two excerpts are untouched sections of the same piece. Listen to all three and pick the one you think was regenerated. You have to choose even when you are unsure.

After you answer, you hear the whole passage the excerpts came from and rate how much it sounds like Ravi's music, from 1 (not at all) to 5 (very much). A comment is optional.

Use headphones and a fixed volume. Your answers are saved in this browser as you go, so you can close the tab and come back. When you finish, press Download responses and send the file.
